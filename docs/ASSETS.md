Some supplied references are portrait or lower-resolution images. `Photo` supports a `position` prop for focal-point adjustments. For wide backgrounds, a landscape image of at least 1920 × 1080 is preferable. For product listings, a clear 4:5 or 1:1 photo is useful.

All visual paths are local. There are no expiring generated-asset URLs or remote-image dependencies.

## Farm exterior fallback prompt

Photorealistic-natural. Wide 16:9 cinematic editorial architectural photograph for a premium agricultural infrastructure website. A believable large modern poultry facility in tropical West African countryside: three long low steel-roof poultry houses receding into the horizon, tall galvanized feed silos beside the nearest building, a gravel service road in the foreground, concrete plinths and natural vegetation. Slightly elevated human-height 24mm camera. Warm dawn light, muted forest greens, silver metal, earth tones and subtle atmospheric haze. Subdued negative space at upper left for website typography; structures visible at right. No text, logos, watermarks, toy buildings or cartoon vegetation.
